Text: With lots of new coworking solutions popping up around the United States, it’s important to know what you need in order to make the most out of your monthly membership. Among the obvious must-haves are amenities that help to create an environment that is not only conducive to work but one that you enjoy going to.

**Electric Car Charging Stations**. If you drive an electric car, this is an obvious perk that some of the most innovative coworking spaces are offering. If you don’t drive one, chances are you will soon. (link: https://www.forbes.com/sites/rrapier/2017/02/05/u-s-electric-vehicle-sales-soared-in-2016/#6430808b217f text: In 2016 global sales of electric cars increased by a monumental 37%).

**Adjustable Desks**. More and more research shows that standing while you work is much healthier than sitting. Being able to adjust your desk will keep you feeling good, offering comfortable options for sitting when needed as well.

**Location**. While chances are you’ll love your coworking space, it’s helpful to have it in a good location in town. Getting hungry? You’ll want to be able to walk out your door to great food options without having to get in your car or go out of your way.

**Lobby Concierge**. Having someone to greet you in the morning makes going to work that much more enjoyable. This service is also practical as it allows shipments a convenient place to be held for you and guests coming to visit will get a good impression, too.

If you live in Baltimore you’re in luck, as there is a coworking solution that provides all of the above. Homebase is growing in popularity thanks to its attention to details, creating an environment its members love working in. Want to see what it has to offer yourself? Book a tour today!
