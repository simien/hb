Title: Home

Intro: Leave it better than we found it.

----

Text: 

Looking for a progressive workspace to meet your entrepreneurial, remote worker or small business needs? Look no further. Homebase is a Baltimore grown coworking space dedicated to providing above and beyond the basic necessities of an office by creating a welcoming atmosphere and a networking culture.

True to its name, Homebase feels like home but creates an atmosphere to get your job done.  Each coworking space has been carefully designed with you in mind.  Windows offer bright, natural light, and the minimalist but comfortable furniture placement and design help to reduce stress.  

Whether you need a space for a day, a week, a month or a year Homebase has you covered through its flexible and affordable rental options.  You can choose to drop in and pick a desk at random or have secured 24/7 access to your own private office space.  Whatever your budget or your needs, Homebase will meet them.

When you choose Homebase you don't have to worry about parking, printing or plugging-in.  Reserved lots, car charging,  top-of-the-line printers, lighting fast wi-fi connections, lobby concierge, and a bounty of local food and drink options are all included with your Homebase package.  We don't want you to worry about anything except for having a good time and getting your job done.
Who needs Homebase?
You, your best friend, your sister, your brother, your old boss.  Everyone needs Homebase.

- Coworking is the way of the future for the United States workforce and is not going anywhere.  It is estimated that by the year 2020, 40% of the working population will be freelancers or solopreneurs and even more will be working remotely.  Large corporations are looking to get rid of costly overhead and more people are pursuing their passions than ever before!
- Other popular locations for remote workers and solopreneurs are not as functional.  Without Homebase people are forced to work from home, the library, coffee shops and hotel rooms. These locations are not conducive to productive work.
- Coworking is more than a place to plug in your laptop.  Workers who have utilized coworking space as part of their professional endeavors report that they feel healthier and happier, as well as more accomplished and more focused on their work!
- Coworking provides a space to collaborate with other driven, experienced professionals that working from home does not offer.

Reserve your business mailing address, permanent office, conference room or flexible workspace with Homebase today. Whatever you choose, you will be able to enjoy every minute of your day around Baltimore’s inner harbor. Book your tour, see the place and join the Homebase community today and start on your way to a more productive work week!
