Title: ValStone Partners opening coworking offices in Clipper Mill, downtown
----
Date: 2018-10-02
----
Text: A private equity firm is opening coworking offices at historic buildings it owns downtown and in the Clipper Mill complex.

ValStone Partners opened a coworking office called Homebase on the fourth floor of 300 W. Pratt St. this week and is looking to open another location at the Clipper Mill complex in Woodberry, near Hampden, by the end of the year.
