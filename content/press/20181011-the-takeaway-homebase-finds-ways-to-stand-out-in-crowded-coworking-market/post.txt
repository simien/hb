Title: The Takeaway: Homebase finds ways to stand out in crowded coworking market

----

Date: 2018-10-11

----

Text: After years spent at startups and in coworking spaces up and down the East Coast, Simien Antonis-Parr is back in Baltimore and helping to launch the city’s newest coworking space: Homebase. Funded by ValStone Partners, Homebase has one location already open at 300 W. Pratt St. with another set to open in Clipper Mill by the end of the year.
